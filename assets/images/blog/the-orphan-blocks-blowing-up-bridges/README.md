# Article assets

Article: **The orphan (blocks) blowing up bridges**, 2026-10-07.

`hero.png` is an editorial illustration generated with the built-in imagegen
tool. It is not evidence or an explorer screenshot. `social.png` is its
1200 × 630 crop. The two square WebP cards preserve the complete illustration.

The four explanatory figures were rendered with Matplotlib in the site's
navy, coral, and blue palette. Text, labels, and numbers are deterministic;
none of the charts was generated by an image model.

- `orphan-flow.png`: a schematic of a source fork and an independently
  committed destination payment. It is not a timing trace.
- `experiment-results.png`: the six rows in `batches.csv`, from the
  author's retained August 2026 experiment records. The article links the
  source fork pages, representative destination fills, and control refund.
  Rows are conditioned on initial source reorgs; five deposits per row
  share one transaction and fork outcome. The output total is gross
  exposure, not an audited final loss.
- `reorg-pattern.png`: the rounded figures in `reorg-pattern.csv`,
  attributed to [CPerezz's July 2026 study](https://ethresear.ch/t/is-the-slot-0-reorg-cost-fixable-epbs-attestation-deadline-study/25338).
  82% is the complement of the rounded 18% Upbit-associated share.
  Orphan rates and operator shares use different denominators.
- `confirmation-policies.png`: a qualitative comparison based on
  [Geth subscriptions](https://geth.ethereum.org/docs/interacting-with-geth/rpc/pubsub),
  [FCR documentation](https://fastconfirm.it/), and
  [Ethereum consensus documentation](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/).
  Its timing labels are typical descriptions, not service guarantees.

The `responsive/` files are 640px and 1280px WebP delivery variants. The
full PNG figures remain available for detailed reading.
