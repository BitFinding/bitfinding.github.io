[the simulation explainer](https://bitfinding.com/blog/enforcing-transaction-simulation/),

The body is approximately 27% shorter.

The source review rechecked Geth's removed-log semantics, Across's
inventory-funded fill and later settlement, Solidity's timestamp variable,
MEV-Boost's minimum-bid fallback, the historical Data Always attribution,
the slot-0 study, and both EthPandaOps FCR studies against their primary sources.

later ones: batch 1 is timestamped +5 seconds, batch 2 +7 seconds, and batch 4

therefore avoids saying that all relayers paid before the next source block.

Explorer pages blocked automated retrieval during this revision. The experiment

## Cover research and direction

Primary research on visible synthetic-image artifacts includes
[Kamali et al.'s guide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08651) and
[Mathys et al.'s taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.06398v1).
These discuss inconsistent geometry, lighting and shadows, text, and semantic
relationships. Appearance alone is not a reliable authorship test.

Editorial examples reviewed were Pentagram's project write-ups for
[MIT Technology Review](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-technology-review)
and [The Baffler](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-baffler).
The chosen direction uses a spare composition, a clear conceptual relationship,
flat colors, and printed edges. The cover is generated artwork, not a copy
of either publication's illustration.

## Current cover generation prompt

Built-in imagegen; opaque background; no reference images in the initial
generation. A refinement used the generated draft as its sole reference.

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: editorial cover illustration for a blockchain security research article, landscape 1536x1024.
Primary request: Original flat cut-paper and two-ink screenprint illustration about a missing source ledger entry and a payment that remains in another ledger.
Composition: a large spare conceptual editorial illustration. Two broad, graphic ledger strips occupy the center, one at left and one at right, separated by generous negative space. The left strip has a single rectangular entry cut out, leaving a clear paper-colored hole; the removed coral-red rectangle sits below, offset and rotated slightly. A thin coral printed line crosses from that missing entry to the right ledger, where a bold blue circular payment mark remains solid. The rest of the ledger marks are a few simple black bars, without writing. The empty cutout and surviving circular mark are the main focal points. All important elements sit in the central 70% so landscape and square crops work.
Medium: flat editorial printmaking and cut-paper collage, matte solid shapes, confident irregular ink edges, restrained authentic ink coverage variation; precise visual relationships; generous negative space; visually clear at small size. Only sparse texture within inked forms.
Palette: clean off-white paper #f4f1e9, near-black ink #15202b, coral #ef576e, and blue #24a2ee. High contrast.
Constraints: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, fake stamps, titles, slogans, watermark, arrows or infographic labels. No realistic bridge, no metallic blocks, no coins with embossed currency symbols, no 3D render, bevels, glow, gradients, volumetric light, fog, stars, planets, particles, floating sci-fi scenery, dramatic clouds, symmetrical panoramic composition, faux antique paper, all-over speckled noise, or decorative details. This should look like a deliberate modern magazine illustration rather than a fantasy scene or stock technology artwork.
```

### Refinement prompt

```text
Edit only the rendering style of the reference image. Preserve the two ledger strips, the missing rectangular entry on the left, the displaced coral rectangle below it, the coral connecting line and the solid blue circular payment mark on the right. Preserve the number and arrangement of these elements.
Flatten the illustration into a modern editorial screenprint: remove all cast shadows, cutout inner shadows, photographic paper grain, bevels, depth, and lighting gradients. Background must be a clean uniform off-white (#f4f1e9), not aged paper. Ledger strips are flat off-white outlined by bold near-black ink marks; use only very sparse dry-ink irregularities within black areas and slightly imperfect printed edges. Coral and blue must be flat matte ink. The hole in the left ledger is completely flat negative space, not a three-dimensional hole. Keep the composition spare and legible. No text or decorative additions.
The result should read as a deliberate flat magazine illustration, with graphic relationships and negative space doing the work.
```
